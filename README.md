There is no commercial eVTOL aircraft designed in Africa, for Africa. That is the problem I am solving.

I founded **Aerospace Alkemists** to build electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft from the ground up — engineered in Nairobi, shaped by the infrastructure realities of African cities, and built to carry people across corridors that roads and railways cannot serve efficiently.

This is not a research project. It is an aircraft company.

---

## Aircraft Programs

**AX-1 — Multi-Passenger eVTOL**
- 8 permanent magnet synchronous motors (PMSM)
- 800V DC bus architecture
- NMC 811 battery system
- Triple-redundant flight control computers (FCCs)
- Target corridor: Nairobi CBD to Thika Town

**AXP-1 — Single-Seat Prototype**
- Derived from the AX-1 via mass-fraction scaling methodology
- Primary flight-test and validation platform
- Used to verify propulsion, control laws, and structural assumptions before production
