## About

I founded **Aerospace Alkemists** to build electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft from the ground up — engineered in Nairobi, shaped by the realities of African cities, and designed to move people across corridors that roads cannot serve.

This is not a research project. It is an aircraft company.

Alongside the aircraft programs, I work in **data engineering** — building the database systems, pipelines, and analytical tooling that underpin modern aviation operations at scale.
